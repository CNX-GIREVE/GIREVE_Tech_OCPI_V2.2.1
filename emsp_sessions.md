# `Sessions Module Specifications`

IOP follows the OCPI standard for Sessions sent by a CPO. [See OCPI 2.2.1 specifications.](https://github.com/ocpi/ocpi/blob/release-2.2.1-bugfixes/mod_sessions.asciidoc)

## Session: Object IDs

The location_id, EVSE_uid, and connector_id fields within the session object are mapped to the IDs provided by the CPO platform. The uniqueness of these IDs is guaranteed by the combination of the country_code and party_id of the CPO in the body of the request.

The id field of the session object corresponds to the ID generated by Gireve and not the ID from the CPO platform.

## Session: ‘VAT’

By default, Gireve will transmit the total_cost as provided by the CPO, including both excl_vat and incl_vat.

## Session: ‘SmartCharging’

The “Charging Preferences” object is not implemented by IOP. therefore, eMSPs cannot send “Charging Preferences” on behalf of the driver.

## PULL Sessions ToIOP: Get List Pagination

Pull Session ToIOP is not currently implemented.

### Information and Requirements

-  Unlike Locations and Tariffs where IOP forwards CPO Ids to eMSPs, Session Ids and authorization references are replaced by Gireve ones. This decision has been made to ensure the consistency with other protocols and Gireve systems.
  
-  Gireve billing feature doesn’t consider or calculate VAT. In case costs are calculated by Gireve and included in Sessions/CDRs, VAT information is not filled.

-  The “Charging Preferences” object is not implemented by IOP. therefore, eMSPs cannot send “Charging Preferences” on behalf of the driver.
